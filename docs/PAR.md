## 2. Game rules

- Line wins pay left to right from reel 1 on consecutive reels; highest win per line only. Line pays are multiples of the **line bet** (total bet / 25).
- **Singularity** (wild) appears on reels 2–4 only and substitutes for all symbols except **Fractal Gate** and **Energy Orb**. It has no pay of its own.
- **Fractal Gate** (scatter) pays anywhere in multiples of **total bet**. Line and scatter wins add.
- 3 / 4 / 5 scatters award 8 / 12 / 20 free spins. Retriggers add the same amounts.
- Free-spin multiplier starts at 1×, applies to all wins of the spin, and rises by 1 after every free spin that pays, up to 10×.
- Free spins use their own reel strips (no orbs). Engineering cap: 1000 free spins per feature (hit 0 times in this run).
- **Energy Orb**: 6+ anywhere on a base-game spin start **Orbit Lock** with 3 respins. Orbs hold; each new orb resets respins to 3.
- Each orb shows a credit prize or a Mini/Minor/Major jackpot. Filling all 15 positions also awards the Grand.
- Fixed jackpots (× total bet): Mini 10, Minor 25, Major 100, Grand 1000.
- If a base spin triggers both features, Orbit Lock plays first, then free spins. Every outcome is decided before reels animate.
- Celebration tiers (presentation only): Big ≥ 15×, Mega ≥ 40×, Epic ≥ 100× total bet.
